# One Sentence Summary

Modern AI has become extremely good at measuring similarity.

Future AI must become equally good at recognizing structure.

**Recognition should precede Similarity.**

---

# The Central Question

Traditional AI asks:

> **How similar are these two objects?**

SRMS asks a different question first:

> **What are these objects?**

Only after structural identity has been recognized does similarity become a reliable tool.

---

# The Core Principle

Traditional AI typically follows:

```text id="i2jlwm"
Input
    ↓
Embedding
    ↓
Similarity
    ↓
Ranking
    ↓
Decision
```

SRMS proposes:

```text id="8n4rdu"
Input
    ↓
Structural Recognition
    ↓
Critical Delta Detection
    ↓
Runtime Activation
    ↓
Structural Confidence
    ↓
Similarity Ranking
    ↓
Decision
```

Recognition establishes the computational world.

Similarity optimizes inside that world.

# Final Message

The goal of this repository is **not** to replace metric similarity.

The goal is to place similarity into a broader computational architecture in which structural recognition comes first.

Reliable AI requires both.

Recognition establishes identity.

Similarity optimizes within recognized structures.

That is the central idea of **Structural Recognition above Metric Similarity (SRMS)**.
